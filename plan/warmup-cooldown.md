# Warm-Up & Cooldown (use every training day)

## Warm-up — 5–8 minutes, before every Push/Pull/Legs/Conditioning day

1. 2 min easy cardio — brisk walk, easy bike, or marching in place
2. 10x arm circles each direction
3. 10x band pull-aparts (or shoulder rolls if no band)
4. 10x bodyweight hip openers (standing knee-to-chest into a controlled
   lunge-and-rotate)
5. 10x bodyweight squats, slow and controlled, no added load
6. For Push days, add: 5x slow scapular push-ups
7. For Pull days, add: 5x scapular pull-ups/retractions (shoulder blades
   pull down and back before you attempt a full rep)
8. For Legs/Conditioning days, add: 10x walking lunges (bodyweight, no
   jump), 10x ankle circles each foot

## Cooldown — 5 minutes, after every training day

Hold each stretch 20–30 seconds per side, breathe normally, no bouncing:

- Chest/doorway stretch
- Overhead triceps/lat stretch
- Standing quad stretch
- Seated or standing hamstring stretch
- Hip flexor lunge stretch
- Child's pose or seated forward fold for lower back

On Conditioning days, walk for 2–3 minutes to bring heart rate down before
stretching.

## Conditioning day heart-rate guidance

Estimate max heart rate as `220 − 48 = 172 bpm`. For the interval and
run-based conditioning days in this plan, aim to stay in the **moderate
zone (roughly 60–75%, ~103–129 bpm)** during "steady" portions, and it's
fine to briefly touch **80% (~138 bpm)** during the harder intervals — but
if you're gasping and can't speak in short phrases, back off the pace. A
smartwatch or basic HR monitor makes this easy to track; if you don't have
one, use the "talk test" instead.
