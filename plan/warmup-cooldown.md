# Warm-Up & Cooldown (use every training day)

## Warm-up — 5–8 minutes, before every Push/Pull/Legs/Conditioning day

1. 2 min easy spin on the stationary bike, low resistance
2. 10x arm circles each direction
3. 10x shoulder rolls / band pull-aparts if you have a band
4. 10x bodyweight hip openers (standing knee-to-chest into a controlled
   lunge-and-rotate)
5. 10x bodyweight squats, slow and controlled, no added load
6. For Push days, add: 5x slow scapular push-ups
7. For Pull days, add: 5x arm swings/scapular retractions (squeeze
   shoulder blades together and hold 2 seconds, arms relaxed at sides)
8. For Legs/Conditioning days, add: 10x walking or reverse lunges
   (bodyweight, no jump), 10x ankle circles each foot

## Cooldown — 5 minutes, after every training day

Hold each stretch 20–30 seconds per side, breathe normally, no bouncing:

- Chest/doorway stretch
- Overhead triceps/lat stretch
- Standing quad stretch
- Seated or standing hamstring stretch
- Hip flexor lunge stretch
- Child's pose or seated forward fold for lower back

On Conditioning days, spin easy on the bike for 2–3 minutes to bring heart
rate down before stretching.

## Conditioning day heart-rate guidance

Estimate your max heart rate as `220 − your age`. For example, at 45 that's
~175 bpm; at 55 that's ~165 bpm. For the bike-based conditioning days in
this plan, aim to stay in the **moderate zone (roughly 60–75% of max)**
during "steady" portions, and it's fine to briefly touch **~80%** during
the harder intervals — but if you're gasping and can't speak in short
phrases, ease off the resistance or cadence. A smartwatch or basic HR
monitor makes this easy to track; if you don't have one, use the "talk
test" instead.
