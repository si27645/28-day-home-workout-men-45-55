# Week 1 (Days 1–7) — Baseline

Warm-up (5–8 min) before every training day, cooldown/stretch (5 min) after.
See [warmup-cooldown.md](warmup-cooldown.md).

### Day 1 — Push
3 rounds, 90 seconds rest between rounds:
- 10 push-ups (elevate hands on a bench/counter if a full push-up isn't
  clean yet)
- 8 walking lunges per leg (bodyweight, no jump)
- 20-second plank

### Day 2 — Pull
3 rounds, 90 seconds rest between rounds:
- 5 pull-ups (or band-assisted pull-ups, or 8 inverted rows)
- 15 dead bugs (slow, controlled — 7–8 per side)
- 12 glute bridges

### Day 3 — Rest / Mobility
Full rest. Optional 20–30 min easy walk + the cooldown stretch routine.

### Day 4 — Legs (low-impact)
3 rounds, 90 seconds rest between rounds:
- 20 air squats, controlled tempo
- 10 step-ups per leg (knee-height step/box — no jump squats this week)
- 20 slow flutter kicks (or marching leg raises if lower back is sensitive)

### Day 5 — Conditioning (moderate)
15-minute interval session:
- Alternate 1 minute brisk walk/easy jog with 1 minute moderate effort
  (bike, rower, or fast incline walk)
- Every 3rd minute, add 5 step-back burpees (no jump) instead of a cardio
  interval
- Keep effort in the moderate zone — see the heart-rate guidance in
  warmup-cooldown.md

### Day 6 — Optional Easy Day
20–30 minutes easy walk, swim, or bike. Skip entirely if sore from the week.

### Day 7 — Rest
Full rest. Foam roll major muscle groups if available.
