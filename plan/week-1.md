# Week 1 (Days 1–7) — Baseline

Warm-up (5–8 min) before every training day, cooldown/stretch (5 min) after.
See [warmup-cooldown.md](warmup-cooldown.md). Use a light-to-moderate
dumbbell/kettlebell weight throughout Week 1 — this week is about
establishing clean form, not testing your max load.

### Day 1 — Push
3 rounds, 90 seconds rest between rounds:
- 10 push-ups (elevate hands on a sturdy chair/counter if a full push-up
  isn't clean yet)
- 8 walking lunges per leg, holding a light dumbbell in each hand
- 20-second plank

### Day 2 — Pull
3 rounds, 90 seconds rest between rounds:
- 10 dumbbell bent-over rows (both arms together, hinge at the hips,
  flat back)
- 15 dead bugs (slow, controlled — 7–8 per side)
- 12 glute bridges

### Day 3 — Rest / Mobility
Full rest. Optional 10–15 min easy bike spin + the cooldown stretch routine.

### Day 4 — Legs (low-impact)
3 rounds, 90 seconds rest between rounds:
- 20 air squats, controlled tempo
- 10 step-ups per leg (sturdy step/stair/chair — no jump squats this week)
- 20 slow flutter kicks (or marching leg raises if lower back is sensitive)

### Day 5 — Conditioning (moderate, stationary bike)
15-minute session on the bike:
- Alternate 1 minute easy spin with 1 minute moderate-effort pedaling
  (higher resistance or cadence)
- Every 3rd minute, pause the bike for 5 step-back burpees (no jump)
  instead of a pedaling interval
- Keep effort in the moderate zone — see the heart-rate guidance in
  warmup-cooldown.md

### Day 6 — Optional Easy Day
15–20 minutes easy bike spin. Skip entirely if sore from the week.

### Day 7 — Rest
Full rest. Foam roll major muscle groups if available.
