# Week 3 (Days 15–21) — Density

This is the first week where low-impact jump-squat and standard-burpee
variants are introduced as optional — only add them if Weeks 1–2 produced
no knee, hip, shoulder, or lower-back soreness the following day.

### Day 15 — Push
4 rounds, 60 seconds rest between rounds:
- 14 push-ups
- 10 walking lunges per leg
- 30-second plank

### Day 16 — Pull
4 rounds, 60 seconds rest between rounds:
- 7 pull-ups
- 20 dead bugs (10 per side)
- 16 glute bridges

### Day 17 — Rest / Mobility
Full rest. Optional 20–30 min easy walk + stretch routine.

### Day 18 — Legs
4 rounds, 60 seconds rest between rounds:
- 26 air squats
- 12 step-ups per leg, OR 8 low-height jump squats per leg if pain-free
  through Weeks 1–2 (soft landing, controlled — quality over height)
- 26 slow flutter kicks / marching leg raises

### Day 19 — Conditioning (moderate)
20-minute interval session:
- Alternate 1 minute brisk walk/easy jog with 1 minute moderate effort
- Every 3rd minute, 8 step-back burpees (or full burpees without the jump
  at the top, if the step-back version has felt easy)
- Stay in the moderate zone; this is not a max-effort day

### Day 20 — Optional Easy Day
20–30 minutes easy walk, swim, or bike.

### Day 21 — Rest
Full rest. Foam roll if available.
