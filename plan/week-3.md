# Week 3 (Days 15–21) — Density

This is the first week where low-impact jump-squat and standard-burpee
variants are introduced as optional — only add them if Weeks 1–2 produced
no knee, hip, shoulder, or lower-back soreness the following day.

### Day 15 — Push
4 rounds, 60 seconds rest between rounds:
- 14 push-ups
- 10 walking lunges per leg, holding dumbbells (heavier than Week 1–2 if
  it's felt easy)
- 30-second plank

### Day 16 — Pull
4 rounds, 60 seconds rest between rounds:
- 14 dumbbell bent-over rows (heavier load than Weeks 1–2 if form has
  stayed clean)
- 20 dead bugs (10 per side)
- 16 glute bridges (add a dumbbell across the hips if bodyweight feels easy)

### Day 17 — Rest / Mobility
Full rest. Optional 10–15 min easy bike spin + stretch routine.

### Day 18 — Legs
4 rounds, 60 seconds rest between rounds:
- 20 goblet squats holding a dumbbell at your chest
- 12 step-ups per leg, OR 8 low-height jump squats per leg if pain-free
  through Weeks 1–2 (soft landing, controlled — quality over height)
- 26 slow flutter kicks / marching leg raises

### Day 19 — Conditioning (moderate, stationary bike)
20-minute session on the bike:
- Alternate 1 minute easy spin with 1 minute moderate-to-hard pedaling
- Every 3rd minute, pause for 8 step-back burpees (or full burpees
  without the jump at the top, if the step-back version has felt easy)
- Stay in the moderate zone; this is not a max-effort day

### Day 20 — Optional Easy Day
15–20 minutes easy bike spin.

### Day 21 — Rest
Full rest. Foam roll if available.
