# Week 2 (Days 8–14) — Modest Volume Build

Only advance to Week 2 if Week 1 felt manageable with no lingering joint
soreness. Otherwise repeat Week 1. If Week 1's dumbbell weight felt easy
by the end, you can bump up one increment this week — but not both weight
and reps in the same week.

### Day 8 — Push
3 rounds, 75 seconds rest between rounds:
- 12 push-ups
- 10 walking lunges per leg, holding dumbbells
- 25-second plank

### Day 9 — Pull
3 rounds, 75 seconds rest between rounds:
- 12 dumbbell bent-over rows
- 18 dead bugs (9 per side)
- 14 glute bridges

### Day 10 — Rest / Mobility
Full rest. Optional 10–15 min easy bike spin + stretch routine.

### Day 11 — Legs (low-impact)
3 rounds, 75 seconds rest between rounds:
- 24 air squats, OR 20 goblet squats holding one dumbbell at your chest if
  bodyweight squats feel easy
- 12 step-ups per leg (still no jump squats — build the base first)
- 24 slow flutter kicks / marching leg raises

### Day 12 — Conditioning (moderate, stationary bike)
18-minute session on the bike:
- Alternate 1 minute easy spin with 1 minute moderate-effort pedaling
- Every 3rd minute, pause for 6 step-back burpees instead of a pedaling
  interval
- Stay in the moderate heart-rate zone; no all-out sprinting

### Day 13 — Optional Easy Day
15–20 minutes easy bike spin.

### Day 14 — Rest
Full rest. Foam roll if available.
