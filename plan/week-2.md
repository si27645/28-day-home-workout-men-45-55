# Week 2 (Days 8–14) — Modest Volume Build

Only advance to Week 2 if Week 1 felt manageable with no lingering joint
soreness. Otherwise repeat Week 1.

### Day 8 — Push
3 rounds, 75 seconds rest between rounds:
- 12 push-ups
- 10 walking lunges per leg
- 25-second plank

### Day 9 — Pull
3 rounds, 75 seconds rest between rounds:
- 6 pull-ups (or band-assisted / inverted rows)
- 18 dead bugs (9 per side)
- 14 glute bridges

### Day 10 — Rest / Mobility
Full rest. Optional 20–30 min easy walk + stretch routine.

### Day 11 — Legs (low-impact)
3 rounds, 75 seconds rest between rounds:
- 24 air squats
- 12 step-ups per leg (still no jump squats — build the base first)
- 24 slow flutter kicks / marching leg raises

### Day 12 — Conditioning (moderate)
18-minute interval session:
- Alternate 1 minute brisk walk/easy jog with 1 minute moderate effort
- Every 3rd minute, 6 step-back burpees instead of a cardio interval
- Stay in the moderate heart-rate zone; no all-out sprinting

### Day 13 — Optional Easy Day
20–30 minutes easy walk, swim, or bike.

### Day 14 — Rest
Full rest. Foam roll if available.
