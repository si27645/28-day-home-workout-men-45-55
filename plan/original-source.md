# Original Plan (Reference)

Unmodified summary of the source plan for comparison, from
[gymnasetips.com/28-day-military-workout](https://www.gymnasetips.com/28-day-military-workout/).
Kept here so the substitutions in the other files can be checked against
what they're replacing — this is **not** the plan to follow at 48; see the
week-1.md through week-4.md files instead.

Format: a 4-day push/pull/legs/conditioning rotation, repeated across 28
days, 30–45 min sessions, pull-up bar only, **no dedicated rest days**.

## Week 1: Baseline (Days 1–7)
- **Day 1 (Push):** 4 rounds — 15 push-ups, 10 lunges/leg, 30s plank, 60s rest
- **Day 2 (Pull):** 4 rounds — 6 pull-ups (or 10 inverted rows), 20 sit-ups, 15 glute bridges, 60s rest
- **Day 3 (Legs):** 4 rounds — 25 air squats, 10 jump squats, 30 flutter kicks, 60s rest
- **Day 4 (Conditioning):** 20-min EMOM — min 1: 8 burpees, min 2: 15 mountain climbers, repeat
- **Day 5 (Push):** Day 1 + 2 push-ups/round
- **Day 6 (Pull):** Day 2 + 1 pull-up/round
- **Day 7 (Legs):** Day 3 + 5 air squats/round

## Week 2: Volume Build (Days 8–14)
- **Day 8 (Conditioning):** 25-min EMOM — 10 burpees, 20 mountain climbers
- **Day 9 (Push):** 5 rounds — 18 push-ups, 12 lunges/leg, 40s plank, 45s rest
- **Day 10 (Pull):** 5 rounds — 7 pull-ups, 22 sit-ups, 18 glute bridges, 45s rest
- **Day 11 (Legs):** 5 rounds — 30 squats, 12 jump squats, 35 flutter kicks, 45s rest
- **Day 12 (Conditioning):** 1-mile timed run + 50 push-ups for time
- **Day 13 (Push):** Day 9 + 3 push-ups/round
- **Day 14 (Pull):** Day 10 + 1 pull-up/round

## Week 3: Density (Days 15–21)
- **Day 15 (Legs):** 5 rounds — 35 squats, 15 jump squats, 40 flutter kicks, 30s rest
- **Day 16 (Conditioning):** 30-min EMOM — 12 burpees, 25 mountain climbers
- **Day 17 (Push):** 5 rounds — 22 push-ups, 14 lunges/leg, 50s plank, 30s rest
- **Day 18 (Pull):** 5 rounds — 9 pull-ups, 25 sit-ups, 22 glute bridges, 30s rest
- **Day 19 (Legs):** Day 15 + 5 squats/round
- **Day 20 (Conditioning):** 1.5-mile timed run + 70 push-ups for time
- **Day 21 (Push):** Day 17 + 2 push-ups/round

## Week 4: Test Week (Days 22–28)
- **Day 22 (Pull):** Day 18 + 1 pull-up/round
- **Day 23 (Legs):** Day 19 + 3 jump squats/round
- **Day 24 (Conditioning):** 2-mile timed run (record time)
- **Day 25 (Test):** 2-min max push-ups, 2-min max sit-ups, max pull-ups
- **Day 26 (Recovery):** 30-min walk + 10 min mobility
- **Day 27 (Recovery):** 20 min light bodyweight training at 50% Day 1 volume
- **Day 28 (Final Test):** Repeat Day 1, compare completion time to baseline

Progression rule stated on the source page: volume increases weekly, no
dedicated rest days, resume the sequence (don't skip ahead) if a day is
missed.
