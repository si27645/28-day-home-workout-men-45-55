# Week 4 (Days 22–28) — Consolidate & Assess

The original plan's Day 25 is an all-out 2-minute max push-up/sit-up test
plus a max pull-up test — exactly the kind of fatigued, form-breakdown
effort that causes injuries in an unconditioned 48-year-old body. This
week replaces that with a controlled assessment and a genuine taper, not
a fourth straight week of increasing load.

### Day 22 — Push
3 rounds (reduced from Week 3 — this is a taper), 75 seconds rest:
- 12 push-ups, strict form
- 10 walking lunges per leg
- 30-second plank

### Day 23 — Pull
3 rounds, 75 seconds rest:
- 7 pull-ups (or your Week 3 equivalent)
- 18 dead bugs
- 14 glute bridges

### Day 24 — Rest / Mobility
Full rest. Optional 20–30 min easy walk + stretch routine.

### Day 25 — Legs (light)
3 rounds, 75 seconds rest:
- 20 air squats
- 10 step-ups per leg
- 20 slow flutter kicks

### Day 26 — Assessment (form-first, not max-effort)
Instead of a timed max-rep test, record baseline numbers you can safely
retest monthly:
- Push-ups: as many **strict, full-range** reps as you can do without form
  breaking down (stop the set the moment form degrades — do not grind out
  extra reps)
- Pull-ups (or rows): same rule — stop at the first rep that isn't clean
- Plank: max hold time with a flat back (stop if it sags)
- 1-mile walk or easy jog: record the time, at a pace where you can still
  hold a conversation

Write the numbers down — they're your new baseline for a future cycle,
not a score to chase to failure.

### Day 27 — Recovery
30-minute easy walk + 10 minutes mobility/stretching. No strength work.

### Day 28 — Repeat Day 1
Do the Week 1, Day 1 push workout (3 rounds: 10 push-ups, 8 lunges/leg,
20s plank) and compare how it feels against four weeks ago — should be
noticeably easier with better form, not just faster.

## After Day 28

Pick one:
- Repeat the whole 4-week block, using Day 26/28 numbers as the new Week 1
  baseline (increase Week 1 targets modestly based on what you recorded).
- Take a deload week (Week 1 volume, but only 2 training days) before
  starting a new cycle.
